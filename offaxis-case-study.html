Project Overview

Off-Axis Discussion is an independent product review website targeting the US market, covering home appliances, tech, kitchen equipment, travel accessories, and curated gift guides. The platform's core promise: no paid placements, no affiliate bias — just honest, research-backed verdicts.

Problem

The product review space is dominated by sites that either look outdated, load slowly, or bury the reader in ads and affiliate disclosures before getting to the point. Users want fast answers and clear recommendations — not a wall of disclaimers.

Solution

I designed and developed a clean, content-first review platform prioritizing:

  • Lightning-fast page loads with Next.js and image optimization
  • Clear verdict-first article structure (readers get the answer in 10 seconds)
  • Category-based navigation across Kitchen, Home, Tech, Travel, and Gifts
  • Trending Deals section with live Amazon product cards
  • SEO-optimized article templates for long-tail keyword targeting
  • Trust-first design — no popup ads, clean reading experience

Design Approach

The design philosophy is "editorial clarity" — borrowing from respected publications rather than typical affiliate sites:

  • Dark, high-contrast brand identity that signals authority and independence
  • Typography-first layouts with generous whitespace and readable line lengths
  • Product cards with clean pricing and direct CTAs — no deceptive dark patterns
  • Mobile-first responsive design for readers on phones
  • Structured data markup for rich search snippets

Tech Stack

Next.js Tailwind CSS Vercel Cloudinary SEO Optimization Structured Data

Outcome

Off-Axis Discussion successfully positions itself as a trustworthy alternative to mainstream review sites. The platform drives organic search traffic through well-structured, verdict-first content, with a reading experience that respects the user's time.
