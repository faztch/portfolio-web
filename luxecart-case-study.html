Project Overview

LuxeCart is a premium fashion e-commerce website designed for modern Pakistani consumers who want streetwear and luxury comfort without traveling to international platforms. The site bridges local buying behaviour with globally inspired shopping UX — black-and-white minimalism, clear pricing, and a slick cart experience.

The Challenge

Pakistan's fashion eCommerce market is rapidly expanding, but most local stores suffer from cluttered product pages, outdated design, and checkout experiences that erode trust. LuxeCart needed to feel premium without being inaccessible — affordable luxury for everyday shoppers.

Solution

I designed and built a complete front-end eCommerce experience including:

  • A full-screen hero section with bold typography and a clear CTA
  • A 4-column product grid with hover zoom, category tags, and instant add-to-cart
  • A slide-out cart sidebar with real-time item count, pricing totals, and remove functionality
  • A sale banner section with urgency copy and offer-claim interaction
  • A working contact form with client-side validation
  • Mobile-first responsive layout that works on every screen size

Design Approach

LuxeCart uses a monochrome-first palette — pure black, white, and zinc — that communicates premium quality without relying on colour tricks:

  • Bold typographic hierarchy using clean sans-serif fonts
  • Generous whitespace giving each product room to breathe
  • Rounded corners and subtle hover shadows for a tactile feel
  • Sticky navigation that stays accessible through the full shopping journey
  • Pakistani market context — Urdu-friendly layout, PKR-ready price display structure

Tech Stack

HTML5 Tailwind CSS Vanilla JavaScript Unsplash API DOM Cart Logic

Outcome

LuxeCart delivers a polished, functional shopping experience that feels premium from the first scroll to checkout. It demonstrates how a well-executed front-end can significantly boost shopper trust and cart conversion rates in the Pakistani digital retail space.
